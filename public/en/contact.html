Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Fort et Château has no account, no server and no ads. Your data stays on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Pages, photos and the Bard's tales are built into the app. Only a page's map (Apple Maps) needs a connection to display.

Why does the app ask for my location?

To show the distance to sites, suggest the ones near you and enable “Validate my visit” within 300 m of a site. It is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. You can decline: the rest of the app works.

How do I switch language?

Tap FR / EN at the top of the main menu: the whole interface switches to French or English.

Is there an iPad version?

Yes, the app is designed for iPhone and iPad.

A castle is missing or a detail is wrong.

Write to us at j_david@live.ca: we add sites and fix pages regularly.

Where do the photos come from?

From Wikimedia Commons, under free licenses. Each photo's credit is shown under the image in the app and listed on the photo credits page.
