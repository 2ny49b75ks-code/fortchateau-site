Privacy

Privacy policy

Summary

Fort et Château does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no user account, no server, and includes no third-party analytics, audience measurement or advertising tools. All content (pages, photos, Bard's tales) is built into the app; all the data you generate stays on your device.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show the distance to sites and the sites nearby, and to know whether you are physically near a site (within 300 m) in order to enable “Validate my visit”. This location is processed entirely on your device, is never sent to a server and is not kept beyond the current session. You can decline it or revoke it at any time in iOS Settings: the rest of the app keeps working.

Data stored on your device

  • The sites you have conquered (Passport) and your wish list.
  • Your Castle Duel ranking.
  • Your Sketchbook notes and the photos you add to it from your photo library.

This data is stored locally in the app's space and is never sent to a server or shared with us. If you use a sharing feature (“Confirmed Explorer” card, “My Top 3”), only the generated image you choose to send leaves your device, through iOS's native sharing mechanism. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Downloading the app and, where applicable, the in-app purchase that unlocks every site are handled by Apple. We receive neither your payment details nor your identity: the app only checks with the App Store that the purchase exists.

No account, no tracking

The app requires no account, uses no advertising identifier and includes no third-party analytics, audience measurement or advertising tools. This website uses no tracking cookie or analytics tool; it loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Children

The app collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below will be updated. Last updated: October 29, 2026.
